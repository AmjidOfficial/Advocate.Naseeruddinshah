§ 01THE ADVOCATE

A record built on
public trust

Naseer Uddin Shah is an Advocate of the Supreme Court of Pakistan, practising from Peshawar across the full appellate ladder. The chambers' work spans constitutional writs, criminal defence and prosecution, civil and property disputes, and the specialised state litigation of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

His career is a study in public trust. He served the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa as State Counsel and Assistant Advocate General, prosecuted as Special Public Prosecutor before the Peshawar High Court, and assumed charge as Additional Advocate General of the province in May 2025. Earlier, he led at the Insaf Lawyers Forum as Vice President (KP) and Senior Vice President (Peshawar district).

A product of the University of Peshawar, he reads law alongside economics and Islamic jurisprudence — a combination that shapes the chambers' practical, commercially literate and ethically grounded counsel.
